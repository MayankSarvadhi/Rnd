RND Industries is a Surat-based pharmaceutical merchant exporter committed to delivering high-quality medicines, injectables, and medical consumables to healthcare markets across Africa and beyond. We bridge India's pharmaceutical excellence with the world's healthcare needs through strict quality compliance and international regulatory expertise.

We source exclusively from ISO-certified and WHO-GMP approved Indian manufacturers, ensuring every product we export meets the highest standards of safety, efficacy, and complete documentation traceability.
